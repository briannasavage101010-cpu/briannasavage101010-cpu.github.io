About

Student entrepreneur and creative builder.

I'm Brianna Savage. I combine design, business thinking and AI-assisted development to turn ideas into websites, products and experiences. I'm the founder of Savage Skincare, and I build websites for other people too.

The story

Minnesota to New York.

I'm originally from Minnesota and now based in New York City, where I attend a school focused on entrepreneurship. My interests sit in the overlap between business, AI, beauty, fashion and design — which sounds like a lot of different things until you notice they are all about making something people actually want to pick up.

That is the thread through everything here. I like turning ideas into tangible projects: websites, product concepts, packaging, digital tools, physical artwork. I would rather have a rough version of a real thing than a polished plan for one.

The biggest of those is Savage Skincare — my own company, where I registered the LLC, built the website and store, designed packaging, and have been working through what it takes to actually manufacture a product.

How I work

Honest about the tools.

I use AI constantly — to research, to design, to build and to improve what I have made. I have some coding experience and I work with AI-assisted development tools to build websites and small apps.

I am not an experienced software engineer, and I don't present myself as one. What I am good at is working out what something needs to do, designing it, writing it, and getting a working version in front of people quickly — then being clear about where the edges of that are.

Learning

Mentorship.

Two things that shaped how I think about building a business. Both were learning experiences, not jobs.

Since March 2026

Weekly AI mentorship

Ongoing weekly sessions on AI — what it is good at, where it falls over, and how to use it properly in real projects rather than as a novelty. It is the reason I build the way I do.

Summer 2026

Finance mentorship with a CFO

A summer mentorship with the CFO of a home-services company — roughly ten hours a week with him plus another five on my own, learning finance and spreadsheets properly. It is where the numbers side of running a business stopped being abstract.

Perspective

Five continents, fifteen-plus countries.

A lot of my curiosity about design and manufacturing comes from having seen it in different places. I have looked at fabrics in Bali, visited fashion houses in Paris, and taken a fashion course in Milan.

I have also been inside manufacturing plants in China, Vietnam and India, which changed how I think about where products actually come from — and met a seamstress running her own business in Tanzania, where I also visited microloan beneficiaries through World Encounter and helped distribute final loans.

These are things I have seen and learned from, not professional experience.
