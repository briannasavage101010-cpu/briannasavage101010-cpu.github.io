Before you write

Three things that make my reply more useful

  • What you do, and who for. One sentence is plenty.
  • What you want visitors to do — buy, book, join a list, or get in touch.
  • When you'd like it live, and a rough budget if you have one in mind.

With those three I can usually tell you on the first reply whether I'm a good fit and roughly what it would cost.
