Why me

I have done this for my own business.

I founded Savage Skincare, registered the LLC, and built its website and online store myself — along with the email signup, the marketing automation, the blog and the FAQs. I had to think through the branding, who the customer was, and what the site needed to say before anyone would care.

That is the honest basis for this service. I am a student entrepreneur and a creative builder, not an experienced software engineer. I design, write and build sites using AI-assisted development tools, and I am straightforward about what that means: I can build you a genuinely good website, and I will tell you when something is outside what I can reliably deliver.

I do not have a wall of client logos yet. What I have is my own company, built end to end, and the work on this site.

Fit

Good fit, bad fit.

Yes

This is for you if…

  • You are starting something and need a proper place to send people.
  • You have a site, but it does not explain what you do or how to reach you.
  • You want to be able to update your own words without asking anyone.
  • You would rather work with someone who tells you what they cannot do.

No

It is probably not, if…

  • You need a logged-in app, a marketplace, or custom back-end software.
  • You want a large corporate site signed off by a committee.
  • You are buying a guaranteed jump in sales or search ranking.
  • You need it live tomorrow.

Process [04]

How a project runs.

  1. First · free

    A short conversation

    You tell me what you do and who it is for. I tell you what I would build and what it costs. If I am not the right person, I will say so then.

  2. Then

    Words before pictures

    I work out the page structure and draft the copy first, because the design follows what you are actually saying. You cut anything that is not true.

  3. Then

    Built in the browser

    No flat mockups you cannot click. I build the real thing and send you a link to open on your phone. Two rounds of changes are included.

  4. Launch

    Live, and handed over

    Your domain, your forms, tested on real phones. Then a walkthrough of how to change things yourself, and 30 days of fixes included.

Questions

The obvious ones.

Who owns the site?

You do. Your domain, your accounts, your files. There is nothing of mine you have to keep paying for.

Can I edit it myself?

Yes. Your text and images will be editable without touching code, and I will walk you through how.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, any photos, and an honest answer about what you want visitors to do. No photos? I will tell you what to take.

How do you actually build it?

I design and write it myself and build it using AI-assisted development tools. I am upfront that I am not a software engineer — it is why I am clear about what I will and will not take on.
