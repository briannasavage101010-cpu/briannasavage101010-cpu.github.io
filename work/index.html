Savage Skincare

Live

My own beauty company. I registered the LLC, built the website and online store at savageskincare.com, set up the marketing automation and email signup, wrote the blog and FAQs, and developed the packaging and brand concepts. Read the full case study →

02

Refillable lip bracelet

Concept

A wearable, refillable lip product that solves a small, real problem: women's clothing often has no pockets, so lip products get left behind or lost. The concept combines beauty, convenience, fashion and a satisfying click. In product development — no prototype, formula or finished product yet.

03

Savage Finance

Internal tool

A planning tool I built for my own business with AI-assisted development: adjust price or marketing spend and the projection redraws, so I can see what a decision does before I make it. Built for me, not released.

04

Budget organiser

Private

A family business' budget spreadsheet rebuilt as a simple web app: budget against actual spending, a running transaction list, and a year-over-year view. Built with AI-assisted development and checked line by line against the original spreadsheet.

05

Saved Brain

Private

A personal tool that pulls everything I had saved across Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest into one searchable library, so saved ideas are actually findable instead of buried. Built for my own use.

06

Chalk art

Mockups

Custom storefront window chalk art, run with a friend. We design mockups and pitch them to nearby businesses — seasonal café displays, Halloween themes, butterflies, bakery illustrations. Simple designs quoted from $20, with around $100 typical depending on scope. Shown here as designs and pitches.

07

DECA — Sereine

Earlier project

I started and led a DECA team and wrote a full skincare business plan and pitch under an earlier brand concept called Sereine: customer positioning, pricing, manufacturing costs, packaging and fulfilment research. The groundwork that Savage Skincare later grew out of, and a separate project from it.
